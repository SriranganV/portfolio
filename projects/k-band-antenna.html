01 · Overview

Problem Statement

High-frequency communication systems (like 5G and satellite radars) require small, planar antennas that can operate in high gigahertz frequency bands while maintaining high return loss, low VSWR, and acceptable gains.

Project Purpose

This research project presents the design, parametric sweep, and optimization of a compact planar monopole antenna operating in the K-Band. Using CST Studio Suite, the geometry is iteratively tuned to achieve a resonance point at approximately 23.6 GHz with a return loss S11 of below -10 dB.

02 · My Contribution

Role & Contribution

  • Designed the primary patch geometry, microstrip feed line, and partial ground plane.
  • Set up simulation boundaries, ports, and mesh properties in CST Studio Suite.
  • Conducted parametric sweeps on slot dimensions and ground feed gaps to tune return loss.
  • Analyzed simulated S11, VSWR plots, far-field 3D radiation patterns, and surface currents.
  • Documented research findings and compiled a technical research poster.

05 · Design Configuration

Core Specifications

Compact Dimensions

Footprint of only 20 × 20 mm² with a 1.6 mm substrate thickness, suitable for compact packages.

Main Resonance

Resonates at approximately 23.6 GHz, operating effectively inside high-frequency communications bands.

High Gain

Achieved a maximum simulated gain of 7.27 dBi in the main radiation direction.

Low VSWR

Voltage Standing Wave Ratio (VSWR) below 2, indicating clean impedance matching with 50Ω feeds.
